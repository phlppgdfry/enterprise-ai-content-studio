# Underlord Skill: Professional LinkedIn Clips Extractor

> **Workflow-type:** Herbruikbare Descript Underlord Skill.  
> **Doel:** Zet langere video's (5-15 minuten) automatisch om in 3 tot 5 virale, professionele clips van 45-60 seconden geoptimaliseerd voor LinkedIn.

---

## 1. Waarom deze skill?
Op LinkedIn presteren video's van **45 tot 60 seconden** aanzienlijk beter dan lange demo's. Bovendien kijkt ruim 75% van de LinkedIn-gebruikers video's zonder geluid. Deze skill zorgt voor perfecte hooks, 9:16 of 1:1 layouts en woord-voor-woord ondertiteling.

---

## 2. Underlord Prompt Reeks

### Stap 1: Clip Selectie op basis van Engagement
```text
Underlord, scan this master video and identify the 3 most impactful 45-to-60 second highlights.
Criteria for selection:
1. High information density (clear problem -> concrete software solution).
2. Strong hook in the first 3 seconds without pleasantries.
3. Actionable takeaway for software engineers or IT managers.
Create a new composition for each extracted highlight.
```

### Stap 2: Formaat Conversie naar LinkedIn Aspect Ratio
```text
Convert this composition to a 1:1 square or 9:16 vertical mobile aspect ratio.
Apply smart auto-framing so that the presenter webcam is positioned in the top third and the live software demonstration occupies the lower two-thirds.
```

### Stap 3: Hook & Headline Banner
```text
Add a bold static header banner across the top of the video with a punchy hook (max 8 words in capital letters, e.g., 'HOE JE AUTOMATISCH VIDEO DEMO'S BOUWT').
Use dark slate background (#0F172A) with crisp white text.
```

### Stap 4: Viral Word-by-Word Captions
```text
Generate animated word-by-word captions positioned centrally below the video demo.
Font: Inter Bold / Helvetica, 48pt.
Active word highlight: Electric Green (#10B981) or Neon Blue (#38BDF8).
Auto-correct any spoken acronyms (API, MCP, LLM, CLI).
```

### Stap 5: CTA & Loop Transition
```text
Trim the final pause immediately after the closing sentence to allow for seamless video looping on LinkedIn.
Add a 1.5-second watermark card at the top-right corner with 'Full link in comments 👇'.
```
